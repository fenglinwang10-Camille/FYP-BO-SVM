The mapping comparison shows differences in search progress as well as in
final scores. At evaluation 20, the normal and Gamma mappings have a mean
best-so-far validation accuracy of 87.45\%, compared with 87.20\% for the
uniform mapping. At evaluation 50, the uniform mapping has the highest
mean, at 88.90\%, followed by normal at 88.50\%, Gamma at 88.35\%, and
exponential at 88.15\%. Thus, the ranking depends on the evaluation budget;
the uniform mapping does not lead throughout the search. These observations
apply to the specified mappings and five runs, not to the distribution
families in general.
