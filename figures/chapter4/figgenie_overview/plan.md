# Drawing plan

Canvas: 480 × 260 pt (169.3 × 91.7 mm). Body labels are 8 pt, arrow annotations and legend labels are 7 pt, and the container title is 9 pt. The height leaves room for the isolated test route and a legend below the diagram.

Regions: data partitioning on the left (x 8–96); one very pale blue BO container in the centre (x 118–368); final configuration and evaluation on the right (x 388–472); a two-entry legend below. White canvas and dark text. Solid dark-blue (#365F82) arrows denote processing and evaluation; dashed muted-orange (#B86B32) arrows denote parameter selection and feedback. Node borders are thin blue. No decoration, icons, shadows or gradients.

Placement: the data column contains four stacked boxes labelled with EEG segments. The split sends training segments directly into Filtering and windowing; its held-out branch follows the left outer edge. The optimiser has Candidate parameters at the top; Filtering and windowing, Feature extraction, and Standardisation and RBF-SVM in a middle row; mean validation accuracy and GP/EI on a lower row. The two final configuration boxes and test evaluation are vertically aligned at right. The held-out data arrow follows the bottom outer edge. All label baselines and wrapping use FigGenie's font metrics through place_text.place; no outlines replace editable text.

Routes: straight and orthogonal arrows, consistent arrowheads. Candidate-to-filtering and candidate-to-classifier edges are dashed orange and carry short parameter labels. Classifier-to-validation is labelled Majority voting. Validation flows left to GP/EI; its selected-configuration branch rises outside the container, labelled After budget exhaustion. GP-to-candidate follows the lower and right perimeter of the container before returning across the top. Selected configuration flows through refit to final evaluation. Held-out test segments follow the left and bottom outer edges and enter only the final evaluation box from below. No connector crosses another connector or passes through a label.

Checks: (1) exactly two arrow styles are used and the legend explains them; (2) every directed connection has a visible arrowhead; (3) the test branch has no optimiser endpoint; (4) labels remain readable at 169 mm print width; (5) no text overflows a box or crosses a route; (6) no external font reference remains in delivered SVG. The caption is supplied outside the image.
